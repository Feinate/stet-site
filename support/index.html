Support

Write to support@stetnote.app. We reply within 5 working days. Never send us the text of your pages: we don't need it to help you.

Why can't I change yesterday's page?

That's the idea of Stet. Until midnight, today's page is yours to change. At midnight (in the time zone where you started the page), it closes for good. Changing your phone's clock doesn't reopen it.

I wrote offline. Is my page lost?

No. Your pages are saved on your phone first. They are sent to your account when you're back online, even after midnight, as long as you wrote them before midnight.

I want a page to disappear

A closed page can't be edited, but it can be removed. Write to us from your account's email address with the date of the page. Deleting your account removes all your pages.

I changed phones

Install Stet and sign in with the same method as before (Apple, Google or email). Your journal is restored automatically. Without an account, pages written on the old phone can't be recovered.

I lost access to my email address

We can't read your journal, so we can't use it to verify who you are. If you signed in with Apple or Google, recover that account first. Otherwise, write to us from any address and we'll see what we can safely do.

Subscription and refunds

Subscriptions are managed by the App Store or Google Play: see our Terms of Sale to cancel or ask for a refund.

Get a copy of my journal

Write to support@stetnote.app from your account's email address. We send you your pages in JSON and plain text within one month.

If you're going through something hard

Stet isn't monitored and nobody reads your pages. If you are in danger, call the emergency services (112 in Europe, 911 in North America) or a crisis line in your country.
