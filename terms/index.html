4. The midnight rule

  • Today's page can be written and changed until midnight in the time zone where the page was started.
  • At midnight, the page closes for good. Neither you nor we can change or reopen it afterwards. Days without writing stay blank; a past day cannot be filled in later.
  • Changing your phone's clock or time zone does not reopen a closed page. If you wrote offline before midnight, what you wrote is kept and synced later.
  • If you really need a page to disappear, you can ask us: see our support page. Deleting your account deletes all your pages.

This rule is an essential feature of Stet, not a defect. By using Stet, you accept it.
